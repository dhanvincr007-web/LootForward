ANTI-FRAUD WORKFLOW

From your inventory to a verified community.

The workflow is intentionally transparent: collect evidence, validate the source, match the need, then release the contribution.

01

Declare

Provide the exact hardware or monetary contribution details before anything enters the queue.

02

Evidence

Attach a proof snapshot or funding statement so the audit layer has supporting evidence.

03

Match

Your contribution is connected to a selected verified recipient and its published needs.

04

Audit

A temporary ticket identifies the pending audit while the contribution awaits human review.
